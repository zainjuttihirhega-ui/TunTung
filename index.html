The Meaning of Sahur

To understand the character's name, it is necessary to understand sahur itself.

Sahur is the meal eaten before dawn during Ramadan. Muslims who are fasting typically eat sahur before the daily fast begins.

In some communities, people have traditionally used sounds, drums, or other forms of signaling to help wake people for the pre-dawn meal.

The Tung Tung Tung character takes inspiration from this idea.

The modern meme, however, adds a fictional and humorous layer that should not be confused with the actual religious practice.
